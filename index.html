About me

I’m a software engineer who likes the whole path a request travels, from the button someone taps to the database row it changes.

I studied Computer Engineering at Pamukkale University in Türkiye, then completed a Master’s in Computer Science at the University of Salerno, specialising in the Internet of Things and graduating with 108/110. Along the way I’ve worked in development teams in both countries, and today I’m a software engineer at BlueNet S.r.l., building web platforms, admin interfaces and the APIs that connect them to other systems.

Working across languages, countries and codebases has made me quick to learn new tools and careful about the things that make software last: clear interfaces, honest documentation, and code the next person can pick up.
